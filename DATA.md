# Data guide

Research datasets and generated products are intentionally excluded from this
public code archive. The original rain-server project remains the authoritative
location for authorized working data.

The workflows reference:

- GPROF V7 and V8 GMI orbital retrievals;
- MRMS radar and Radar Quality Index fields;
- Stage IV precipitation analyses;
- ERA5 and MERRA-2 reanalysis fields;
- IMERG precipitation products;
- AutoSnow surface snow/ice information; and
- derived footprint matchups, gridded summaries, validation tables, and figures.

Obtain each dataset from its authorized provider and follow its applicable terms.
Large collocation archives, diagnostic CSV files, plots, and logs must remain
outside version control.
